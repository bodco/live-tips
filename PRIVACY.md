# Pacenote privacy policy

Updated 15 November 2026. The full text is on the website: **[pacenote.bodco.pm/privacy/](https://pacenote.bodco.pm/privacy/)** ([українською](PRIVACY.uk.md)).

In short:

- **Audio stays on your Mac.** Speech is recognised locally with Whisper; audio is not sent to any AI service. It is written to disk only when you record a call (below).
- **Call recording (0.9.5), off by default.** If you turn it on in Settings > Call recording or choose it for a call, Pacenote saves the call's sound, or the sound and the screen (a whole screen or one window, never Pacenote's own windows), as a file in the project's `video/` folder on your Mac. It is never sent to an AI. It goes to Google Drive only when you upload it, or when you choose to upload every recording: into your own Drive (My Drive > Pacenote > the project), with the `drive.file` permission that lets Pacenote see only the files it made. You can move or share that folder in Drive; Pacenote keeps writing into it. If you turn on Transcript in Drive (0.11.0, off by default), the call's transcript (speaker names, their lines and times, the title, date, project and participants; no tips or notes) goes as a text file into the same folder. Nothing is shown or played to the other participants: telling them is up to you (Settings > General > Recording consent can say or copy a message).
- **Transcripts, voice prints, settings and logs** stay in your project folders, the app's folder and the macOS Keychain.
- **Text leaves the Mac only to services you set up with your own keys or accounts:** Anthropic, through your API key or your Claude account with Claude Code, for tips, preparation, search and the wrap-up; OpenAI, if selected in Settings > AI, through your API key for live tips and after-call jobs (Responses API, `store: false`), or through your ChatGPT account using the official Codex CLI for preparation, tips and after-call work (ephemeral sessions, CLI-managed credentials); Google Gemini, if selected in Settings > AI or turned on as the backup when Claude or ChatGPT is out, through your own API key of a paid-tier project, for preparation, tips and after-call jobs (the classic streaming API, no stored interactions; Google does not use paid-tier content to improve its products, free-tier content can be used, so use a paid-tier key); your Google account through Google's Antigravity CLI, if you choose it for the preparation or after the call (or as the backup with no Gemini key): Google's Antigravity terms let Google use your conversations, the call text included, to improve its research and products and let people at Google review them, Telemetry off does not stop storage, and the terms forbid use through third-party software such as Pacenote, which may lead Google to suspend the account; Pacenote removes agy's local copy of each conversation it made; Notion for the meeting page; HubSpot, if you connect it, for the CRM update you approve after a call; web pages you name in the preparation; one-time model and package downloads (Hugging Face, GitHub, PyPI).
- **HubSpot (0.8.7).** The service key stays in the macOS Keychain. Pacenote reads the company, the deal, contacts and recent activity into a local snapshot next to the brief. It writes a note, deal fields and a task only after you tick them in Update HubSpot and press Apply; the transcript goes into the note only if you turn that on (off by default).
- **Google (0.9.1).** If you connect Google accounts, Pacenote reads their calendars, the Google Docs, Sheets and Slides you link in a call's setup or that are attached to the invitation, and the Google Meet participants and transcript of a call when you ask for them. Another Drive file (a PDF or Word file) is read only when you confirm that one file in Google Picker with Confirm in Google (0.10.0); Pacenote never has access to the rest of your Drive. It writes only a new Google Doc when you export notes there, and the call recordings (and, if you turn it on, transcripts) you upload to Drive. Copies of the documents stay in the project folder on your Mac; their text goes to the AI service of the preparation like your other files. The tokens stay in the macOS Keychain; Remove revokes them. Pacenote's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements; it is not used for advertising, not sold and not used to train models.
- **Pacenote account and server (0.11.0; calls since 0.12.0), optional.** Settings > Account can sign you in to the Pacenote server with Google. Signing in gives Pacenote only your Google email, name and picture; the server has no access to your Drive, Calendar or Docs, and this sign-in is separate from the Google accounts you connect for calendars and Drive. The server keeps your account and the list of your signed-in devices (name, app version, last activity). Since 0.12.0, for each project where you turn on Sync to Pacenote server (Settings > Projects > the project's menu; off by default), the app sends after each call, and again when its notes or Meeting Sheet change: the call's title, time, length, type, audience and language, its project, the call notes as the summary, decisions, tasks and the Meeting Sheet, plus links to your recording and transcript in Google Drive. A call's page can leave that one call out. Participants' names and emails and the consent record are not sent yet; this policy will say so before they are. Audio, video, transcripts, voice prints and the text of live tips never go to the Pacenote server. The server and its backups are in the EU (Hetzner, Finland); the content of your calls is encrypted with a key of your own, kept apart from the database. The sign-in tokens stay in the macOS Keychain. Sign out on this Mac or everywhere, or delete your server data, in Settings > Account at any time: your content key is destroyed at once and the rest is removed within 30 days, also from backups. Without signing in, nothing changes.
- **Organizations (later).** If you join an organization in Pacenote, its team leads and department heads will see the summaries, decisions, tasks and call details of the people they manage, as the organization sets it up; admins manage people but do not read call content. Recordings and transcripts in Google Drive are seen by whoever has access to that Drive folder, which you or your organization decide in Google Drive.
- **Company web search before sales calls (0.8.7).** On by default, switch it off in Settings > AI. Only the company's name or domain goes into the search, never anything from your files; the brief says what was searched.
- **Local usage log (0.8.7).** Pacenote keeps a log of which features you use (event names and numbers, never the words of a call, names or titles) in the app's folder, one file a month, 12 months kept. It stays on your Mac; switch it off in Settings > General > Privacy.
- **Help improve Pacenote (0.9.4; on by default since 0.10.1).** Unless you turn it off in Settings > General > Privacy (or in the last onboarding step), the usage log above is sent to the author once a week: event names and small numbers of whole days since you turned it on (for example how many tips you rated useful, fine but off the point or not useful, and the reason chip), the app and rules versions, the interface language and which way to the AI each job uses. Never the words of a call, the text of tips or of your edits to them, your comments on tips, names, projects, files or titles: what Pacenote learns from your edits and ratings stays on your Mac. If you turned the usage log itself off, sharing stays off. Updating from an earlier version switches sharing on once and tells you so at the next launch. A random id made on your Mac when you turn it on tells your batches apart from other people's; it is forgotten when you turn it off. The batches go to a Cloudflare Worker of the author that keeps no IP address; Show what is sent opens the next batch before it goes.
- **Otherwise nothing is sent to the author** (the Pacenote account above is the only exception, and only if you sign in). No analytics SDK, no tracking, no crash reporting. Nothing is sent to clients by itself.
- **You are responsible for the people on your calls:** tell them you transcribe, record if you do, and use an AI assistant, and get consent where it is required. Summaries and Meeting Sheets can hold confidential or personal information about them and your clients: send to the Pacenote server only what you and your organization may store there.
