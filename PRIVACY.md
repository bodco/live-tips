# Pacenote privacy policy

Updated 5 November 2026. The full text is on the website: **[pacenote.bodco.pm/privacy/](https://pacenote.bodco.pm/privacy/)** ([українською](PRIVACY.uk.md)).

In short:

- **Audio stays on your Mac.** Speech is recognised locally with Whisper; audio is not sent to any AI service. It is written to disk only when you record a call (below).
- **Call recording (0.9.5), off by default.** If you turn it on in Settings > Call recording or choose it for a call, Pacenote saves the call's sound, or the sound and the screen (a whole screen or one window, never Pacenote's own windows), as a file in the project's `video/` folder on your Mac. It is never sent to an AI. It goes to Google Drive only when you upload it, or when you choose to upload every recording: into your own Drive (My Drive > Pacenote > the project), with the `drive.file` permission that lets Pacenote see only the files it made. Nothing is shown or played to the other participants: telling them is up to you (Settings > General > Recording consent can say or copy a message).
- **Transcripts, voice prints, settings and logs** stay in your project folders, the app's folder and the macOS Keychain.
- **Text leaves the Mac only to services you set up with your own keys or accounts:** Anthropic, through your API key or your Claude account with Claude Code, for tips, preparation, search and the wrap-up; OpenAI, if selected in Settings > AI, through your API key for live tips and after-call jobs (Responses API, `store: false`), or through your ChatGPT account using the official Codex CLI for preparation, tips and after-call work (ephemeral sessions, CLI-managed credentials); Google Gemini, if selected in Settings > AI or turned on as the backup when Claude or ChatGPT is out, through your own API key of a paid-tier project, for preparation, tips and after-call jobs (the classic streaming API, no stored interactions; Google does not use paid-tier content to improve its products, free-tier content can be used, so use a paid-tier key); your Google account through Google's Antigravity CLI, if you choose it for the preparation or after the call (or as the backup with no Gemini key): Google's Antigravity terms let Google use your conversations, the call text included, to improve its research and products and let people at Google review them, Telemetry off does not stop storage, and the terms forbid use through third-party software such as Pacenote, which may lead Google to suspend the account; Pacenote removes agy's local copy of each conversation it made; Notion for the meeting page; HubSpot, if you connect it, for the CRM update you approve after a call; web pages you name in the preparation; one-time model and package downloads (Hugging Face, GitHub, PyPI).
- **HubSpot (0.8.7).** The service key stays in the macOS Keychain. Pacenote reads the company, the deal, contacts and recent activity into a local snapshot next to the brief. It writes a note, deal fields and a task only after you tick them in Update HubSpot and press Apply; the transcript goes into the note only if you turn that on (off by default).
- **Google (0.9.1).** If you connect Google accounts, Pacenote reads their calendars, the Google Docs, Sheets and Slides you link in a call's setup or that are attached to the invitation, and the Google Meet participants and transcript of a call when you ask for them. It writes only a new Google Doc when you export notes there, and the call recordings you upload to Drive. Copies of the documents stay in the project folder on your Mac; their text goes to the AI service of the preparation like your other files. The tokens stay in the macOS Keychain; Remove revokes them. Pacenote's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements; it is not used for advertising, not sold and not used to train models.
- **Company web search before sales calls (0.8.7).** On by default, switch it off in Settings > AI. Only the company's name or domain goes into the search, never anything from your files; the brief says what was searched.
- **Local usage log (0.8.7).** Pacenote keeps a log of which features you use (event names and numbers, never the words of a call, names or titles) in the app's folder, one file a month, 12 months kept. It stays on your Mac; switch it off in Settings > General > Privacy.
- **Help improve Pacenote (0.9.4), off by default.** Only if you turn it on in Settings > General > Privacy, the usage log above is sent to the author once a week: event names and small numbers of whole days since you turned it on (for example how many tips you rated useful, fine but off the point or not useful, and the reason chip), the app and rules versions, the interface language and which way to the AI each job uses. Never the words of a call, your comments on tips, names, projects, files or titles. A random id made on your Mac when you turn it on tells your batches apart from other people's; it is forgotten when you turn it off. The batches go to a Cloudflare Worker of the author that keeps no IP address; Show what is sent opens the next batch before it goes.
- **Otherwise nothing is sent to the author.** No analytics SDK, no tracking, no crash reporting. Nothing is sent to clients by itself.
- **You are responsible for the people on your calls:** tell them you transcribe, record if you do, and use an AI assistant, and get consent where it is required.
