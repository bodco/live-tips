**Methods** (default `general`):
- `general` General sales (general): No formal qualification. Pacenote follows the client's pain, the value for them, objections, and whether there is a next step with a date. Criteria: `pain` Pain, `value` Value, `objections` Objections, `next_step` Next step.
- `bant` BANT (qualification): Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline: is there money, the person who decides, a real need and a time frame. Criteria: `budget` Budget, `authority` Authority, `need` Need, `timeline` Timeline.
- `spiced` SPICED (qualification): Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical event, Decision: the client's context, what hurts, what it costs, the deadline that forces a decision and how they decide. Criteria: `situation` Situation, `pain` Pain, `impact` Impact, `critical_event` Critical event, `decision` Decision.
- `meddic` MEDDIC (qualification): Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion: for larger deals with several people involved. Criteria: `metrics` Metrics, `economic_buyer` Economic buyer, `decision_criteria` Decision criteria, `decision_process` Decision process, `identify_pain` Identify pain, `champion` Champion.
- `meddpicc` MEDDPICC (qualification): MEDDIC plus Paper process and Competition: for complex deals with procurement, legal review and rival vendors. Criteria: `metrics` Metrics, `economic_buyer` Economic buyer, `decision_criteria` Decision criteria, `decision_process` Decision process, `paper_process` Paper process, `identify_pain` Identify pain, `champion` Champion, `competition` Competition.
- `gpctba` GPCTBA/C&I (qualification): Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline, Budget, Authority, Negative consequences, Positive implications: starts from the client's goals rather than from money. Criteria: `goals` Goals, `plans` Plans, `challenges` Challenges, `timeline` Timeline, `budget` Budget, `authority` Authority, `negative_consequences` Negative consequences, `positive_implications` Positive implications.
- `spin` SPIN (questioning): Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff: a sequence of questions that lets the client say the value in their own words. Criteria: `situation` Situation, `problem` Problem, `implication` Implication, `need_payoff` Need-payoff.
- `sandler` Sandler pain funnel (questioning): Goes from the surface problem down to its cost and what it means to the person, then checks they want to fix it now. Criteria: `surface_problem` Surface problem, `specifics` Specifics, `history` History, `cost` Cost, `personal_impact` Personal impact, `commitment` Commitment.
- `challenger` Challenger (approach): Teach, Tailor, Take control: the seller brings an insight that changes how the client sees the problem, fits it to this person and leads to a decision. Criteria: `teach` Teach, `tailor` Tailor, `take_control` Take control. Judged on the seller's actions.

- Tip `pain`: the client named a pain without its size or consequence; ask about the scale or what happens if nothing changes
